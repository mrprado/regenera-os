# Regulatory model

Canonical spec: docs/master-spec.md parts XLIV–XLVIII. Built so far: the capital compliance gate (M2, see
docs/capital-model.md). Next (M4): jurisdictions, the jurisdiction matrix, project requirements and permits,
regulatory reviews on any subject, KYC status.

Principles already enforced:
- The OS never states that anything is compliant. It records who concluded what, when, on what evidence, with what
  conditions.
- Project regulation (host-country permission to build) and capital regulation (offering, solicitation, promotion,
  intermediary activity) are separate tracks; lender and investor standards (IFC Performance Standards, World Bank ESF,
  Equator Principles) sit with the capital source.
- No autonomous regulated outreach: every message needs human approval, and investment communications also pass the
  gate at send time.
